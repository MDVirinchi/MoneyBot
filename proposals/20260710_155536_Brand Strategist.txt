# Brand Strategy for Digital's Social Growth

Building authentic community on TikTok and Instagram requires more than viral content—it needs a clear identity that resonates with your specific audience. Most brands miss this and chase trends instead of building something defensible.

I've helped 3 DTC brands grow from 50K to 200K+ engaged followers by starting with audience insight, not aesthetics. We mapped their core audience psychology, created 3-4 content pillars that actually reflected their brand values, and used paid social strategically to amplify what was already working organically.

For Digital, I'd start by auditing your current audience composition and content performance, then build a 90-day content strategy with specific themes and posting cadence before we touch paid media.
