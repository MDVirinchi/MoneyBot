# Brand Strategist Proposal

Digital brands often struggle with authentic community growth because they chase algorithms instead of audience psychology. The real win is building content pillars that *feel* native to each platform while consistently reinforcing your brand identity.

I've helped 3 consumer brands grow engaged TikTok followings from zero to 50K+ in 6 months by starting with audience research, then designing content themes that creators (and the algorithm) naturally gravitated toward. The secondary paid layer then amplified what was already working organically.

For your project, I'd start by:
1. Mapping your ideal audience across platforms
2. Identifying 3-4 content pillars that differentiate you
3. Creating a platform-specific content playbook (TikTok and Instagram operate very differently)

From there, we'd build the paid strategy around what's proven to resonate.

**Let's hop on a quick call to discuss your current audience composition and where you want to be in 90 days.**
