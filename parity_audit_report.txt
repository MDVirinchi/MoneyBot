  OPS : avg_vol = np.nanmean(vol_ep)                        [line 144]
  Detail: FV excludes zero-volume days before computing the average. OPS uses np.nanmean which includes zero entries (NaN excluded, zeros included). Lower avg_vol in OPS → more days satisfy vol[i] > 1.5 × avg_vol → higher EP scores → different ranking.   Impact: Systematic EP score inflation. Estimated 1-3 rank position shifts.
  Fix   : Replace np.nanmean(vol_ep) with mean of vol_ep[vol_ep > 0].
